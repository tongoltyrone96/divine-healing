Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This notice covers this website, strawberryhope.com. The Divine Awakening app has its own privacy policy.

What this website collects

The website has no sign-in and no forms, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

It uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). When you visit, the pixel uses cookies and similar technology to send Meta information such as the page you viewed, whether you tapped a button to download the app or book a session, your IP address, and details about your browser and device.

Why

To measure how well our Facebook and Instagram ads work, and to show our ads to people who are likely to find them useful, including people who have visited this site before. We do not sell this information.

Meta handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Change how Meta uses your activity for ads in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works the same without them.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at aboutads.info or youradchoices.ca.

Booking and the app stores

The booking buttons open Calendly, and the download buttons open the App Store or Google Play. Those services have their own privacy policies.
